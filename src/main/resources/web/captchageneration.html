What Tesseract (both engines) handles vs. fails

Decodes reliably: slight rotation (up to ~10°), mild wave, sparse noise dots, thin straight interference lines, spaced characters, dark text on light background.
Borderline: rotation 15–25°, strong wave, moderate overlap, colored text with low contrast, dotted characters.
Usually fails: heavy character overlap, warped/wavy strokes through glyphs, complex backgrounds, very tight letter spacing, rotation above ~35°.
